iosESP

The one-board ESP32 Wi-Fi bridge for WarBLE Go. iPhones can't scan Wi-Fi — this board does it for you and streams every network it hears to the app over Bluetooth. No pairing, no setup, no terminal.

Get WarBLE Go on the App Store free · iPhone

Flash it from your browser

  1. Plug any BLE-capable ESP32 (C3, C5, C6, S3…) into a computer over USB.
  2. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on that computer (iPhone and Safari can't talk to USB).
  3. Hit the button, pick the board's port, and let it run. It detects the chip and flashes the matching build — about a minute.
Your browser can't do USB flashing — use Chrome or Edge on a computer, or the manual steps below. This page must be served over HTTPS for USB flashing.

Board doesn't show up in the port list? Hold its BOOT button while plugging it in, then try again.

  1. Power the board. In WarBLE Go turn the Wi-Fi bridge on once — it links by itself from then on.

Or flash it yourself

Download iosESP.zip prebuilt bins for ESP32-C3 · C5 · C6 · S3, plus the source for any other BLE-capable ESP32 (the S2 has no Bluetooth)

Unzip, pick your chip's folder under firmware-bins/, then (replace <chip> and <PORT>):

esptool --chip <chip> -p <PORT> erase_flash
esptool --chip <chip> -p <PORT> -b 460800 write_flash 0x0 wardrive-bridge-merged.bin

One merged file at 0x0 — the offsets are baked in (the C5's bootloader lives at 0x2000; the merged image already handles that). Building from source/: idf.py set-target <chip> && idf.py build flash.
