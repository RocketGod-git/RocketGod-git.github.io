iosESP

The one-board ESP32 Wi-Fi bridge for Wardrive Go on iPhone. iPhones can't scan Wi-Fi — this board does it for you and streams every network it hears to the app over Bluetooth. No pairing, no setup, no terminal.

Before you start

  • A computer — Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS. Flashing doesn't work from an iPhone or iPad.
  • Chrome or Edge on that computer. Safari and Firefox can't talk to USB devices.
  • A USB data cable. Many cables only charge; if the board never shows up, try another cable first.
  • Close anything using the board — Arduino IDE, a serial monitor, another flasher tab.
  • Drivers: boards with native USB (most C3, C5, C6 and S3 boards, including the XIAO) need none. Boards with a separate USB chip may need its driver on Windows or macOS: CP210x or CH340 / CH343.

Flash it from your browser

  1. Plug the ESP32-C3, C5, C6 or S3 board into the computer over USB.
  2. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on that computer.
  3. Hit the button and pick the board's port from the list. It usually reads USB JTAG/serial debug unit (native USB), CP210x or USB-SERIAL CH340 — on a Mac, cu.usbmodem… or cu.usbserial…. If several appear, unplug the board, see which one disappears, then plug it back in.
  4. The page detects the chip, erases it and flashes the right build. It takes about a minute — keep the board plugged in until the console says Done.

If it doesn't work

  

    

      
The board isn't in the port list

      
Try another USB cable (it may be charge-only), another USB port, or plug in directly instead of through a hub. Boards with a CP210x or CH340 chip need that driver installed first (see above). On Linux, add yourself to the dialout group and log back in.

      
"Failed to connect", timeouts, or it stops at "Connecting…"

      
Put the board in download mode: hold its BOOT button (sometimes labeled B or IO0), tap RESET — or unplug and replug it while holding BOOT — then release BOOT and press the flash button again. The port name can change after this, so pick it again.

      
"The port is busy" or access denied

      
Another app or browser tab has the board open. Close Arduino IDE, serial monitors and other flasher tabs, or unplug and replug the board, then try again.

      
The button is grayed out

      
The browser can't flash over USB. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer, or the manual steps below.

      
It flashed, but the app doesn't find it

      
Press the board's RESET button or replug it. Make sure Link to the board is on and Bluetooth is allowed for Wardrive Go in iOS Settings. If it still won't link, flash it once more — the page erases the board first, which clears anything left over from other firmware.

Or flash it yourself

  

    

      Download iosESP.zip
      prebuilt bins for ESP32-C3 · C5 · C6 · S3, plus the source
    

    
Unzip, pick your chip's folder under firmware-bins/, then (replace <chip> and <PORT>):


esptool --chip <chip> -p <PORT> erase_flash
esptool --chip <chip> -p <PORT> -b 460800 write_flash 0x0 wardrive-bridge-merged.bin
