Flash it from your browser

  2. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on that computer.
  3. Hit the button and pick the board's port. It detects the chip and flashes the right build in about a minute.

Or flash it yourself

  

    

      Download WardriveESP.zip
      prebuilt bins for ESP32-C3 · C5 · C6 · S3, plus the source
    

    
Unzip, pick your chip's folder under firmware-bins/, then (replace <chip> and <PORT>):


esptool --chip <chip> -p <PORT> erase_flash
esptool --chip <chip> -p <PORT> -b 460800 write_flash 0x0 wardrive-bridge-merged.bin

    

      
Biscuit Pro or Ultra second chip (the ESP32 on the CH340 port): grab wardrive-coproc-merged.bin, flash it, then send WDCP setboard biscuit_ultra (or biscuit_pro) on that port at 115200.


esptool --chip esp32 -p <CH340 PORT> -b 460800 write_flash 0x0 wardrive-coproc-merged.bin
